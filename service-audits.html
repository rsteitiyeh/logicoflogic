03/ai integration audits

Read by humans, written for action.

What this is.

Independent assessment of an existing AI integration. Where it's helping. Where it's theatre. Where it's quietly broken — silently degrading or producing outputs nobody is checking. The audit looks at the system end-to-end: prompts, models, tooling, monitoring, the humans in the loop.

How it runs.

Two-week engagement. Read the code, talk to the team, measure the metrics that exist and define the ones that don't. No pre-formatted template. The audit follows what the system actually does, not what a generic AI assessment framework expects.

Findings are ranked, specific, and actionable. Each one names what to do, who would do it, and roughly how long it takes.

What you get.

An audit report that anyone on the team can read and act on. Specific. Ranked. Signed. The follow-up implementation can be picked up by the existing team or, where wanted, by LogicOfLogic under service 01.
