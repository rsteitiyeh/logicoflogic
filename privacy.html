Privacy Policy

This policy is a working draft pending attorney review. The substance is accurate; the legal language will be tightened before publish.

1. Who we are

LogicOfLogic is an operating brand of Trilot LLC, a Wyoming, USA limited liability company (re-registered 2024). Trilot LLC is the data controller for personal data described below. Privacy questions: privacy@logicoflogic.com.

2. What data we collect

Form submissions on this site capture name, email, selected interests, and message text. Calendar bookings via Cal.com capture name, email, and time zone. Newsletter and waitlist sign-ups via MailerLite capture email and an opt-in record. Cloudflare records server logs (IP, user agent, request timestamps) for security and abuse prevention; this layer is cookieless.

3. Why we collect it

Lawful bases under GDPR: legitimate interest (security, abuse prevention, basic analytics), consent (cookies, marketing email), and contract performance (responding to your booking or inquiry).

4. Third-party processors

This site uses three named processors directly, each operating under a Data Processing Agreement:

  • MailerLite — US-based email service provider for newsletter and waitlist. EU/UK transfers governed by Standard Contractual Clauses. Stores email, name, and selected interest tags.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — privacy-respecting bot protection on the waitlist form. Replaces traditional CAPTCHAs. Does not use Google. Minimal signal collection, no cross-site tracking.
  • Cal.com — booking calendar embedded on the contact page. Sets cookies on its own iframe domain when used, governed by Cal.com's own privacy policy.

Cloudflare also provides hosting, security, and cookieless web analytics for the site itself.

5. Data retention

Form submissions are retained for 24 months unless you request earlier deletion. Newsletter and waitlist subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe. Calendar bookings are retained for 12 months after the meeting.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK) you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Under CCPA (California) you have rights to know, delete, opt out of sale (we do not sell data), and non-discrimination. Email privacy@logicoflogic.com to exercise any right; we respond within 30 days.

7. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States. Where required, transfers are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

This site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

Material changes are posted here with a revised date. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

privacy@logicoflogic.com for any privacy question. Postal: Trilot LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States.
