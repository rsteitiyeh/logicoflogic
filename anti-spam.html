Anti-Spam Policy

This policy is a working draft pending attorney review. The substance is accurate; the legal language will be tightened before publish.

1. Our commitment

LogicOfLogic does not send unsolicited bulk email. All email marketing is consent-based.

2. How you opt in

By direct subscription via the waitlist form on logicoflogic.com — newsletter signup, lead magnet, cohort interest, or agency engagement. We use double opt-in: after submission, a confirmation email arrives via MailerLite, and your subscription only activates once you click that confirmation. We keep records (timestamp, source, selected interests).

3. What we send

The weekly LogicOfLogic field-notes newsletter and occasional updates relevant to the interests you selected (cohort, agency, general). Cross-promotion with sister brands (Trilot, DnBlackbook) only with explicit consent.

4. Opt-out

Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link via MailerLite. Unsubscribes take effect immediately on click. The legal SLA under CAN-SPAM is 10 business days; GDPR is "without undue delay." We honour them well within both.

5. Sender identification

Every email identifies LogicOfLogic / Trilot LLC as the sender, includes the physical postal address (Wyoming), and states the purpose.

6. Reporting abuse

If you received an email from us you did not request, reply with "abuse" or email abuse@logicoflogic.com. We investigate within 5 business days.

7. Third-party tools

MailerLite is our email service provider. It maintains its own anti-abuse practices. The waitlist form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated signups.

8. Compliance

This policy aligns with CAN-SPAM (US, 15 USC §7701 et seq.) and GDPR Article 21 (right to object).
