What Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you subscribe to our email list or download our resources, we collect:

  • Email address

  • Date and time of subscription
  • The form or page where you subscribed

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically:

  • IP address

This information is collected through cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To deliver the resources you requested (such as the Prompt Cheat Sheet PDF)
  • To send you weekly AI education emails you opted in to receive

  • To understand how visitors interact with our site
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you voluntarily subscribe to our email list by checking the consent checkbox and submitting your email address. You may withdraw consent at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Legitimate Interest: For website analytics and security purposes, where our interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
