04/cohort & team training

Trained on your codebase.

What this is.

Training engagements for company teams. Built around the team's actual codebase, the team's actual workflows, and the team's actual constraints. Not a generic AI workshop. Not a YouTube playlist with a certificate at the end.

How it runs.

Four-to-six-week program. A mix of live sessions and async work. Hands-on with the team's real systems — under NDA, with read-only access where appropriate. The work is paired: the team builds, the LogicOfLogic engagement guides, reviews, and corrects.

What you get.

A team that actually uses AI in its work — not just one early adopter who attended the conference. A documented playbook for the team's specific stack. Eval and review patterns the team owns. Cohorts run privately for now; a public cohort opens once the private engagements have stress-tested the curriculum.
