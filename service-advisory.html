02/ai infrastructure advisory

AI as core infrastructure.

What this is.

Strategy and architecture for companies adopting AI as core infrastructure, not as a feature bolted onto an existing product. Vendor short-listing. Build-vs-buy decisions. Data architecture for retrieval. Model selection across families and price points. The long view on what to commit to and what to keep replaceable.

How it runs.

Direct engagement with the operator and the technical leadership of the company being advised. Written deliverables, not slide decks. Decisions documented as they're made, with the reasoning visible and the trade-offs named.

Engagements run four to twelve weeks depending on scope. Often this work feeds directly into an implementation phase under service 01.

What you get.

A written technical strategy, dated and signed. A vendor short-list with trade-offs annotated. A three-month rollout plan with the dependencies surfaced. Anyone who joins the project later can read the document set and understand why the decisions look the way they do.
