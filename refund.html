Refund Policy

This policy is a working draft pending attorney review. The substance is accurate; the legal language will be tightened before publish.

1. Scope

Most LogicOfLogic engagements are services, not products. Service engagements (the four offerings on this site — agent design & implementation, AI infrastructure advisory, AI integration audits, cohort & team training) are governed by their separate written engagement agreements, which contain the cancellation, milestone, and payment terms specific to that engagement. This policy covers any digital product LogicOfLogic may publish, and the newsletter / lead-magnet flow.

2. Newsletter and lead-magnet downloads

The newsletter and the prompt cheat sheet are free. There is no payment, so no refund applies. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

3. Future digital products

Should LogicOfLogic publish a paid digital product (course, paid cohort, paid resource pack), the standard policy will be: refunds within 14 days of purchase, conditional on the buyer having consumed less than 30% of the content. Consumption measured via platform progress tracking. Each product page will reiterate the applicable terms at point of sale.

4. Service engagements

The terms of any service engagement — including cancellation, work-in-progress payment, and dispute handling — are set out in the signed engagement agreement for that specific project. Refer to that document. Where the engagement agreement is silent, the default is: paid work-to-date is non-refundable; future-period payments may be cancelled with reasonable notice.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@logicoflogic.com with the order number (or engagement reference) and reason. We respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.

6. EU consumer rights

EU/EEA consumers retain statutory rights including the 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Rights Directive (where applicable). This policy meets or exceeds that baseline for any consumer-facing digital product we publish.
