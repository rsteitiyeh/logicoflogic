What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

How We Use Cookies

Logic of Logic (logicoflogic.com) uses a minimal number of cookies, primarily for essential website functionality, security, and email form operation. We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioral tracking.

Your Cookie Choices

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, Search, and Services → Cookies

Blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Cookie Consent (EU/EEA Visitors)

If you are visiting from the European Union or European Economic Area, we will present you with a cookie consent banner on your first visit. Essential cookies required for website security and basic functionality do not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive.
