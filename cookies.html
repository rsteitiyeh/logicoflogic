Cookie Policy

This policy is a working draft pending attorney review. The substance is accurate; the legal language will be tightened before publish.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser to remember information between visits. Some are required for the site to work; others enable analytics or marketing.

2. What we set on logicoflogic.com

Strictly necessary. A localStorage key (logicoflogic.cookieConsent) for your cookie-banner choice (technically not a cookie, disclosed for completeness), and a Cloudflare security cookie (__cf_bm) for abuse prevention.

Bot protection. The waitlist form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-respecting CAPTCHA replacement. It does not use Google reCAPTCHA and does not set persistent tracking cookies.

Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless — no cookies are set. We do not use Google Analytics.

Functional. Cal.com sets cookies on its own iframe domain when you interact with the calendar. Those are governed by Cal.com's own cookie policy.

Marketing. None at this time. MailerLite tracking is opt-in via the email link tracker (server-side, not cookies set by this site).

4. How to control cookies

Use the cookie banner on logicoflogic.com to accept or decline. Browser settings can clear or block cookies entirely. Clearing localStorage will reset the banner.
